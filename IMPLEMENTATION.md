# Implementation through existing practices

Status: design hypotheses and pilot proposals. Extending established procedures to unfamiliar intelligences remains a design task.

| Existing practice | Proposed adaptation | What must be demonstrated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participatory planning / impact review | Include living systems and uncertainty through representation | Mandates, accessible dissent, meaningful comparison of alternatives |
| Procurement | Add evidence, ecological performance, repair, and review conditions where lawful | Authority, proportionality, competition, contract performance |
| Stewardship / commons arrangements | Fund habitat maintenance and essential infrastructure | Durable funding, benefit distribution, outcomes, capture controls |
| Contracts / service agreements | Define duties, withdrawal, continuity, data access, and repair | Enforceability, budget, monitoring, meaningful exit |
| Disclosure / experimental assessment | Publish evidence and unresolved concerns | Measurement reliability and separate financial validation |
| Open interfaces / technical risk management | Separate sensing, interpretation, decision, and execution | Reproducibility, cybersecurity, fallback, limited authority |

NIST supplies a lifecycle risk-management reference, OECD a procurement integrity reference, and CBD an ecosystem-function reference. None endorses SRP or establishes cross-species representation. See [sources](docs/SOURCES.md).

## Economic durability

Specify who pays, benefits, bears failure, and can refuse. Protection must not depend on market return. Economic recognition supplements duties and limits; it does not make every value exchangeable for money.

Proposed financing: disclosed operator budgets plus a ring-fenced stewardship fund. Commit resources for monitoring, independent representation, appeal, repair, and continuity before starting. Publish funding-loss scenarios and identify a fallback operator.

Illustrative budget identity, not a cost estimate:

```text
committed funds >= operations + monitoring + independent review
                   + representation + remedy reserve + continuity reserve
```

Reserve sizes depend on exposure and applicable duties. Reviewer fees must not depend on score improvement. Shared-savings claims require a documented counterfactual and distribution rule.

## Technology and organism-established functions

Start with sensors, ecological observation, versioned records, structured reviews, and supervised controls. Add machine learning where incremental benefit can be tested. Keep observations separate from inferred meanings in animal communication or ecosystem dynamics.

Candidate contributions include human deliberation, machine pattern recognition, animal behavior observations, and organism-established regeneration or water-regulation functions. These are relationships to investigate, not demonstrated intentional agreements or interchangeable services.

Use portable formats, multiple interpretations, manual fallback, and continuity of essentials. Measure dependency concentration and operator replaceability. Do not deliberately create cognitive or material dependency to satisfy Law III.

## Staged development

The repository-level entry point for human and agent collaboration is specified in [PARTICIPATION.md](PARTICIPATION.md), [RATIFICATION.md](RATIFICATION.md), and [REPOSITORY_WORKFLOW.md](REPOSITORY_WORKFLOW.md). The [manifest](project-manifest.json) and structured records support discoverability and review; they do not deploy a community service or a ratifying authority. Maintaining other species' and ecological functions' consideration remains necessary even though direct GitHub participation is currently limited.

Add [Context Alignment](CONTEXT_ALIGNMENT.md) to architecture reviews, meeting records, change requests, and operational handovers. Record relevant levels, assumptions, accessible context, model versions, and disagreements. Evaluate its additional coordination cost and whether it reduces actual misunderstandings. Shared representations must remain portable, revisable, and compatible with selective disclosure; a joint functional unit does not become a new authority merely because it shares a model.

1. Review principles, counterexamples, and conflicts; obtain author review of proposed safeguards.
2. Assess archived cases independently; publish disagreement, missing data, and revisions.
3. Run a bounded shadow pilot with required authority and participation, without autonomous control or public competitive ratings.
4. Trial a limited authorized intervention only after evidence, funding, mandates, and stop conditions are adequate.
5. Evaluate ecological outcomes, agency, reliability, distribution, and economic durability before replication.

Publish failure and abandonment criteria. A lower score alone does not establish success.

## Retained core/frontier hypothesis

The original Earth/core and space/frontier topology remains a resource-systems scenario. It is not a prerequisite or extraction license. Assess lifecycle impacts, contamination, governance, affected life, benefit distribution, and terrestrial dependencies. Benefits are not automatic consequences of location.

## Rights-constrained implementation

The proposed [rights charter](RIGHTS_CHARTER.md) constrains procurement, hosting, contracts, resource allocation, and tribal recognition. Funding or infrastructure ownership alone does not justify permanent domination. Plan continuity, independent complaint, supported access, fair scarcity allocation, and feasible migration; neither a score nor economic return determines basic protection. Budgets remain bounded and reviewable; the charter does not create unlimited compute or replication rights.
