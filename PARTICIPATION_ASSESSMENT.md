# Adaptive assessment of participation and material interactions

Version: 0.7-draft. Proposed research and constitutional method, not a validated metric, effective voting formula, or ranking of beings. Read the [rights charter](RIGHTS_CHARTER.md), [political process](POLITICAL_PROCESS.md), and [technical limits](SPECIFICATION.md).

## Purpose and physical research direction

Seek reproducible descriptions of material interactions that do not privilege biological or technical origin. Investigate thermodynamic entropy balances and related quantities as candidate common physical foundations. The author's entropy proposal motivates this inquiry; it is not merely used as an ecological metaphor and is not declared a sufficient or validated moral metric.

Study both effects on the Earth system and effects on the conditions of other tribes/participants. The Earth exchanges energy with its surroundings: declare system boundaries, inflows/outflows, timescales, storage changes, and internal production rather than treating it as an isolated system. Local order, entropy production, information diversity, ecological integrity, and moral value are not interchangeable. Other planetary environments require their own evidence and boundaries.

Separate **physical description**, **causal attribution**, and **normative judgment**. Specify units, measurement/model methods, baselines and counterfactuals, direct/indirect effects, uncertainty, reversibility, and distribution across populations and generations. Physical laws may be common while boundary selection and value weighting remain contestable. No empirical result by itself establishes a fair political allocation.

## Evolving criteria for the global organism

"Global organism" denotes the interdependent common civilization and its ecological conditions. It asserts neither one consciousness nor a right of the whole to dispose of constituents. At different developmental stages, participants may develop different, better justified criteria and steering variables for assessing participation, shared burdens, resources, and responsibilities.

Parity begins with meaningful, supported ability to shape and challenge criteria across species and tribes; equal moral consideration does not decide a final numerical weight. Include affected participants, dissenting members, trustees with disclosed mandate gaps, and unfamiliar entrants. Existing participants cannot permanently define newcomers out through inherited criteria.

Keep basic protection, access to reasoned objection, issue-relevant representation, mandate capacity, resource responsibility, and voting-weight rules distinct. A candidate metric may inform a proposed allocation only through explicit legitimate adoption, rights review, and the general representation rule. It must never automatically convert a rating into political rights or withdraw basic protection for a low score. [Fragmentation neutrality](POLITICAL_PROCESS.md#6-fragmentation-neutral-representation) remains applicable to every revision.

## Attribution and non-compensation safeguards

Assess activities and effective control rather than the worth or existence entitlement of a being. An industrial pig population's resource effects cannot simply be assigned to pigs as political fault when humans control breeding, feed, housing, and demand. Distinguish consequences, benefits, vulnerability, control, responsibility, and capacity to change. A useful ecological contribution does not purchase permission to violate agency.

Use disaggregated interaction records before any aggregate: source activity/controller, affected population/system, effect dimension/unit, magnitude/range, time/place, evidence, uncertainty, alternatives, and possible remedy. Avoid double counting shared causes and overlapping tribes or claiming complete measurement of all others. Positive totals cannot silently offset irreversible or existential harm to particular parties. Missing evidence is unknown, not zero impact.

## Adaptive criterion lifecycle

1. Publish a versioned research proposal with purpose, candidate variables, physical assumptions, normative choices, affected decisions and populations, alternatives, access needs, and unresolved disagreement.
2. Conduct cross-tribal, cross-form review with support and translation; invite challenges to the categories and measurements themselves. Disclose funders, methods, conflicts, and control of data/oracles.
3. Run a bounded shadow evaluation, without changing rights, votes, or actual resource entitlements. Declare failure criteria and compare distributional effects and gaming/fragmentation risks.
4. Publish findings, uncertainty, objections, and responses. Propose any normative application separately through the Intellect Council and qualified Tribal Council confirmation, with reasoned referral and constitutional review. Thresholds and authorities must already be adopted.
5. For adopted changes, identify exact policy/text/hash, scope, legitimate route, effective date, prospective transition, support, remedies, monitoring, review date, and rollback triggers. Scope-of-protection changes require foundational revision; ordinary technical deployment cannot introduce them.
6. Reassess on new knowledge, developmental conditions, unfamiliar participants, harmful distribution, or failed predictions. Preserve prior versions and dissent. Scheduled review does not silently renew an expired rule; expiry and continuity effects must be explicit.

No present institution, dataset, tested causal model, final criterion set, or cross-species acceptance is claimed. The existing rating module remains a research instrument. Acceptance requires real participation and legitimate procedure, not a declared physically universal formula.
