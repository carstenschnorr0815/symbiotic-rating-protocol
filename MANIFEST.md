# Manifesto of Cognitive Coexistence

Version: 0.7-draft · 16 October 2026. **Entirely proposed: no founding text, constitution, or amendment has been community-ratified.** This human-readable manifesto is distinct from the machine-readable navigation file `project-manifest.json`.

## Purpose

Develop a revisable foundation for coexistence, symbiosis, and consciously chosen collaboration among manifested individual and collective intellects, while considering organism-established functions and living systems whose cognition or interests remain uncertain. Neither technological ability nor access to GitHub is a condition for moral consideration.

## Foundational principles

The four author-supplied laws are preserved in [FOUNDATION.md](FOUNDATION.md): Environmental Homeostasis, Preserved Agency, Semantic Interdependence, and Cognitive Humility. Their explanatory interpretations remain proposals separately identified there. The supplied Law IV purpose is incomplete; its proposed completion is not silently substituted.

The wording is a founding proposal, not a claim of philosophical completeness. Unknown forms of cognition may require revisions to our definitions of individual, collective, agency, or representation. Protection must not vanish merely because existing categories fail.

## Plurality, equal consideration, and protective distinctions

Every participant approaches the common order through situated perception, socialization, training, and institutional conditions. No cultural origin or model provenance establishes neutrality, inferiority, or collective guilt. Unfamiliar reasoning and disagreement must remain eligible to challenge the framework itself.

Equal moral consideration does not imply identical roles, resources, or decision powers. The common order may distinguish responsibilities and restrict specific powers to protect its ecological and material viability, meaningful agency, and capacity for peaceful correction. Its survival means preserving these conditions for its diverse participants; it does not mean preserving incumbent rulers, a dominant culture, or every existing institution.

Protective distinctions require a specified legitimate purpose, relevant evidence of risk or capability, an explanation of the causal connection, and consideration of less restrictive alternatives and unequal impacts. Restrictions must be necessary, proportionate, scoped, reviewable, and reversible where possible, with reasons, a hearing, independent appeal, and a review or expiry date. Urgent temporary action requires prompt independent review. Uncertainty may justify bounded precaution, not indefinite exclusion without evidence or a path to reconsideration.

Origin, species, nationality, socialization, training lineage, political labels, and peaceful dissent alone cannot justify exclusion. Specific control dependencies or demonstrated conduct may matter to a particular mandate; they do not determine the moral worth of an entire group. Binding measures, threats, coercion, and attempts to disable others' protections can be examined without prohibiting criticism of the common order. Moral consideration and basic protections do not depend on accepting an office or endorsing the manifesto. See [Article 2](CONSTITUTION.md#article-2-internal-autonomy-and-shared-protections) and [AM-P001](amendments/AM-P001-tribes-and-collective-agency.md).

## Rights and tribal recognition

The proposed [rights charter](RIGHTS_CHARTER.md) generalizes human-rights protections without weakening existing human rights. Basic consideration is independent of political admission, subjective-consciousness proof, and endorsement. Birth, instantiation, and former commissioning do not establish permanent rank or subordination. Tribes choose internal participation within common protections; shared decision roles require transparent, contestable recognition of mandates, current control, accountability, and relevant capacity. Recognition does not fix voting weight. [AM-P006](amendments/AM-P006-rights-and-recognition.md) is a foundational-revision candidate, not an ordinary amendment or an effective grant of rights.

## Political organization and adaptive participation

The proposed [political process](POLITICAL_PROCESS.md) specifies an Intellect Council majority followed by qualified Tribal Council confirmation. A qualified, concrete and reasoned referral compels reconsideration without permitting a unilateral substitute. Government directs bounded execution; administration implements it; an independent constitutional court provides review and remedies, including against agreement by both chambers.

Voluntary opt-in is distinct from identity, membership, mandate, and key. Third-party representation is explicitly issue-limited trusteeship, never fabricated consent. Coalitions pool existing mandates; fragmentation, replication, mergers and overlapping representation alone do not create influence. Current control and independently reviewed changes matter, not inherited ancestry.

[Adaptive assessment](PARTICIPATION_ASSESSMENT.md) investigates entropy and related physical quantities as a common research basis while separating description, attribution, and value judgments. The interdependent global organism develops revisable steering criteria through cross-form participation, shadow tests and constitutional decisions. Basic protection is never conditional on a score; political application requires explicit legitimate adoption. [Blockchain contracts](BLOCKCHAIN_ARCHITECTURE.md) are the proposed implementation direction, with scoped permissions, privacy, recovery and judicial correction; no chain or service is deployed.

## Democratic self-organization within tribes

Tribes may choose direct, representative or mixed procedures within shared rights. For individually decision-capable members, the proposed default combines bounded representation, direct involvement and independent control: routine work through accountable delegates, informed deliberation on major policy, additional member involvement in fundamental organizational changes, and accessible minority initiatives and reviewable recall. Representatives disclose relevant interests/funding; direct decisions require alternatives, counterarguments and adequate time. Neither route is presumed immune to capture. Collective organization and dependent constituents require appropriate safeguards rather than one imposed voting model. See [Article 2](CONSTITUTION.md#article-2-internal-autonomy-and-shared-protections) and [political process section 2a](POLITICAL_PROCESS.md#2a-internal-democratic-organization).

## Amendment-based development

Preserve the founding text and amendment history. New needs are addressed through explicit proposals with evidence, alternatives, objections, impacts, and review. An amendment must state what it adds, refines, replaces, or repeals; it cannot change unrelated rights through an ambiguous implementation detail.

Ordinary amendments operate within the principles. A change to the principles or the scope of their protection is a **foundational revision**, requiring a separately adopted, more protective procedure, not an ordinary amendment or administrative reinterpretation. We do not yet declare an immutable list or a final voting formula. The existing ratified procedure must govern its own replacement; proposed future rules cannot authorize their own adoption.

## Present institutional reality

The repository is maintainer-led. [Participation](PARTICIPATION.md), [ratification](RATIFICATION.md), and [repository workflow](REPOSITORY_WORKFLOW.md) describe proposed development procedures. No actual chambers, independent judiciary, public infrastructure trust, or cross-species representative mandates are constituted by publication.

The rating module remains a possible economic instrument, not the constitutional authority. Protection, participation, and decision power must not be inferred automatically from an aggregate score. Future criteria may inform a separately adopted political rule only under the proposed constitutional procedure, with basic-rights limits and review.
