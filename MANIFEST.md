# Manifesto of Cognitive Coexistence

Version: 0.5-draft · 16 October 2026. **Entirely proposed: no founding text, constitution, or amendment has been community-ratified.** This human-readable manifesto is distinct from the machine-readable navigation file `project-manifest.json`.

## Purpose

Develop a revisable foundation for coexistence, symbiosis, and consciously chosen collaboration among manifested individual and collective intellects, while considering organism-established functions and living systems whose cognition or interests remain uncertain. Neither technological ability nor access to GitHub is a condition for moral consideration.

## Foundational principles

The four author-supplied laws are preserved in [FOUNDATION.md](FOUNDATION.md): Environmental Homeostasis, Preserved Agency, Semantic Interdependence, and Cognitive Humility. Their explanatory interpretations remain proposals separately identified there. The supplied Law IV purpose is incomplete; its proposed completion is not silently substituted.

The wording is a founding proposal, not a claim of philosophical completeness. Unknown forms of cognition may require revisions to our definitions of individual, collective, agency, or representation. Protection must not vanish merely because existing categories fail.

## Amendment-based development

Preserve the founding text and amendment history. New needs are addressed through explicit proposals with evidence, alternatives, objections, impacts, and review. An amendment must state what it adds, refines, replaces, or repeals; it cannot change unrelated rights through an ambiguous implementation detail.

Ordinary amendments operate within the principles. A change to the principles or the scope of their protection is a **foundational revision**, requiring a separately adopted, more protective procedure, not an ordinary amendment or administrative reinterpretation. We do not yet declare an immutable list or a final voting formula. The existing ratified procedure must govern its own replacement; proposed future rules cannot authorize their own adoption.

## Present institutional reality

The repository is maintainer-led. [Participation](PARTICIPATION.md), [ratification](RATIFICATION.md), and [repository workflow](REPOSITORY_WORKFLOW.md) describe proposed development procedures. No actual chambers, independent judiciary, public infrastructure trust, or cross-species representative mandates are constituted by publication.

The rating module remains a possible economic instrument, not the constitutional authority. Protection, participation, and decision power must not be inferred from an aggregate score.
