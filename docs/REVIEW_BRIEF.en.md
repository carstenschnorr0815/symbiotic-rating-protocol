# External review brief: a proposed manifesto of cognitive coexistence

Version: SRP 0.5-draft, 16 October 2026. Prepared for a possible future invitation; nobody has been contacted or appointed by this brief. Record the exact reviewed commit when submitting a response.

SRP explores coexistence among individual and collective intelligences and consideration of living-system functions whose cognition or interests may remain uncertain. The [manifesto](../MANIFEST.md) separates four author-supplied principles, a proposed constitutional order, explicit amendment candidates, and implementation protocols. No founding constitution or amendment has been community-ratified.

The central design problems are agency within and between associations, representation of swarms/nonhuman participants, separation of powers, material and informational independence, and revision when unfamiliar forms challenge existing categories. Two chambers and independent review are proposals, not existing institutions. Ethical consideration, political standing, consent, and technical competence are deliberately distinguished.

For a bounded first review, please select **one** [amendment candidate](../amendments/README.md), rather than trying to endorse the whole project. Useful questions are:

1. Which concept or inference is unsound, untestable, or inappropriately imported from another domain?
2. Which participant or interest is excluded, misrepresented, or exposed to coercion?
3. What concrete counterexample would defeat the proposed rule?
4. What smaller revision or empirical study would make the claim defensible?
5. Which unresolved objection must remain visible even if the proposal proceeds?

Written critical feedback is welcome without agreement with the principles. Indicate scope, evidence, conflicts, and limits. A reviewer recommendation or explanation of why the question is ill-posed can also help. Review participation is not endorsement, political representation, or ratification. Any role, effort/compensation, confidentiality, publication of names/quotes, and reuse should be agreed before work or attribution.

The project currently remains maintainer-led. Technical validators check records, not truth, consciousness, independent consent, or constitutional legitimacy. Preserve disagreement and identify the text version to which any assessment applies. This brief does not request a public support statement or grant anyone authority to speak for a species, tribe, or all AI systems.
