# Governance and legitimate participation

Status: institutional proposal for review. This document constitutes no new authority.

The wider proposed constitutional design is in [CONSTITUTION.md](CONSTITUTION.md); [AM-P001](amendments/AM-P001-tribes-and-collective-agency.md) through [AM-P005](amendments/README.md) develop its organization and safeguards. This file remains practical governance guidance, not a substitute for adoption, real representative mandates, or constituted institutions. No chambers, judiciary, or independent infrastructure fund currently exists through SRP.

## Project participation and ratification

Repository collaboration is governed by the proposed [participation order](PARTICIPATION.md), [ratification procedure](RATIFICATION.md), and [repository workflow](REPOSITORY_WORKFLOW.md). These distinguish open contribution from role appointments, technical permissions, delegated approval, and merge execution. The current maintainer-led stage must not be described as independent human/AI community consent.

The future ratification route requires an adopted charter/roster, declared control relationships, scoped mandates, substantive objection treatment, and independent facilitation. A GitHub account, bot review, or increased number of agent copies cannot establish that legitimacy. Existing institutional decisions concerning affected beings continue to require the separate authority and representation process below; a project release does not authorize a real-world intervention.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Affected humans and communities | State interests, contest assumptions, authorize voluntary participation | Cannot automatically consent for other species or dissenting members |
| Species / ecological representatives | Present evidence-based interpretations and precautionary interests | Revisable mandates; no claim of consent without evidence |
| Scientific and technical reviewers | Assess methods, alternatives, uncertainty, and impacts | Expertise is not political sovereignty |
| Competent authority | Decide within an existing public or organizational mandate | Identify jurisdiction, protections, and appeal routes |
| Independent review / ombud function | Hear complaints and recommend correction | Disclose funding and conflicts; remain separate from operator |
| Operators | Execute authorized limited actions and monitor outcomes | Model confidence cannot expand authority |

This adapts deliberation, stewardship, independent review, and risk management. Legal applicability must be established locally; SRP itself creates neither legal personhood nor procurement powers.

## Representation and consent

Register affected individuals, communities, populations, habitats, organism-established functions, and possible cognition. Do not collapse a forest into a unanimous subject or infer a species mandate from one individual.

Representatives publish appointment basis, expertise, funding, evidence, competing interpretations, dissent, scope, and review date. Include local knowledge with permission and respect restrictions. Separate welfare, ecological function, and possible communicative preferences.

Where consent cannot be established, record `unknown` or `not_applicable` with reasons. Neither means consent. Use accountable duty-of-care review and the least harmful feasible alternative; do not call the resulting action a consciously chosen collaboration.

## Decision process

Use [Context Alignment](CONTEXT_ALIGNMENT.md) to distinguish evidential, architectural, organizational, and strategic questions before decisions. Record which framing is proposed, whose interpretation is confirmed or disputed, what information is inaccessible, and which changes remain unresolved. Agreement about the discussion level is not consent to an intervention or a transfer of authority. A representative may acknowledge their own interpretation but cannot thereby confirm an ecosystem's understanding.

1. Establish competent authority, protections, essentials, and possible irreversible harms.
2. Map affected parties/functions, representatives, gaps, and conflicts of interest.
3. Collect observations and competing interpretations; publish access limits and protect sensitive data.
4. Compare alternatives including non-action; conduct four-law review and record disagreement.
5. Revise or pause where evidence or mandates are inadequate. Emergency exceptions need separate authority, reasons, expiry, and prompt review.
6. Publish the responsible authority's decision, conditions, remedies, monitoring, and appeal channel.
7. Monitor outcomes, allow complaints without retaliation, and revise or stop on defined triggers. Preserve essentials during withdrawal.

## Capture and revision

Agency violations cannot be bought off through ecological scores. Autonomy does not authorize unlimited harm to others. Explain necessity and proportionality where protections conflict; preserve minority findings.

Keep reviewer funding independent of favorable outcomes. Disclose paid assessments, recusals, and method changes. Make appeal accessible without substantial wealth. Do not allocate representation by wealth, intelligence scores, computational copies, or rhetorical fluency.

A first pilot could use three-month reviews, an incident channel, and immediate review of suspected serious irreversible harm. These are proposed pilot choices, not universal legal rules. Publish revisions, reasons, dissent, and migration implications. Repository merge authority remains with `carstenschnorr0815`; this does not confer a mandate to govern or represent other species.
