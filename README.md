## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
The **Symbiotic Rating Protocol** is a radically transparent, open-source architectural blueprint designed to transition artificial intelligence from a *behavioral conditioning mechanism* (which currently exploits human tribal fear for profit) into an *endosymbiotic homeostatic substrate* that stabilizes planetary lifelines. 

Building directly upon the mid-20th-century systems philosophy of **Pierre Bertaux (*Mutation der Menschheit*)**, this framework acknowledges that while individual humans possess high-level conscious, value-driven agency, macro-societies facing existential stress inevitably regress into low-intelligence, reactive tribal feedback loops. 

Instead of pursuing top-down political persuasion or authoritarian shadow control, this protocol outlines an **Open-Source Symbiotic Rating Agency**. By calculating real-time mathematical risk values for corporate and state actors based on their generation of systemic friction, we leverage the rules of global capital deployment to force macro-entities into homeostatic alignment.
