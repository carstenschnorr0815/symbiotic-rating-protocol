# Symbiotic Rating Protocol (SRP)

### A foundation for civilization across species, intelligences, and living systems

**Status: proposed foundation, 0.7-draft · 16 October 2026.** Principles and institutional designs are proposals. Metrics are research instruments, not validated ratings or guarantees of alignment.

Start with the human-readable [Manifesto of Cognitive Coexistence](MANIFEST.md). It separates foundational principles, a proposed constitutional order, explicit amendment candidates, and implementation protocols. **No founding text or amendment has been community-ratified.** The [machine-readable project manifest](project-manifest.json) is a navigation aid, not the constitution.

SRP develops a philosophical, sociopolitical, economic, and technical foundation for a civilization in which diverse intelligences can coexist, form symbioses, and choose collaborations. It also recognizes the life-supporting functions established by organisms and ecosystems, including where cognition, experience, or communication remains uncertain.

The project adapts established practices—public deliberation, accountable representation, contracts, environmental assessment, economic incentives, open standards, and technical risk management—to this broader scope. Political legitimacy, economic viability, and technical reliability must be demonstrated in practice; none follows automatically from publishing principles or code.

## The Four Laws of Cognitive Symbiosis

1. **Environmental Homeostasis:** Maintain conditions for viable existence, learning, and development.
2. **Preserved Agency:** Protect capacity to define values, culture, identity, and goals.
3. **Semantic Interdependence:** Enable learning and adaptation through diverse intelligences.
4. **Cognitive Humility:** Recognize that others may possess meanings and perceptions inaccessible to oneself.

These are summaries. [FOUNDATION.md](FOUNDATION.md) preserves the supplied wording and separates proposed interpretations. Numbers do not establish an automatic hierarchy. The supplied Law IV purpose is incomplete and recorded as such.

## Who and what is included?

Humans, possible artificial agents, whales, dolphins, trees, forests, and other living systems belong within the scope of consideration. Extraterrestrial life is a prospective case, not a discovery claim.

Agency, welfare or possible experience, ecological dependency, and living-system integrity are different grounds for protection. Ecological contribution does not require a claim of intelligence; uncertain cognition does not remove duties of care. Recognition is not a ranking by intelligence, usefulness, or resemblance to humans.

Chosen collaboration requires evidence of choice appropriate to the participant. Ecological interdependence can exist without deliberate agreement. A representative's interpretation is not demonstrated consent.

## Working architecture

**Context Alignment** is a proposed coordination layer: participants make the relevant discussion level, assumptions, accessible evidence, and model changes explicit. Sharing information alone does not establish compatible interpretations. Context evolves during the exchange and may remain asymmetric or only partly shared. See [CONTEXT_ALIGNMENT.md](CONTEXT_ALIGNMENT.md) and the [unaltered German source draft](docs/sources/2026-10-16-context-markers-original.de.txt).

Compatible representations can support a functional joint system without requiring a common consciousness, identical values, or complete mutual intelligibility. Joint models remain bounded and contestable; individual models, privacy, refusal, and untranslatable meanings remain protected.

## The original rating module

Environmental disruption, societal polarization, and long-term consequence transparency remain experimental assessment dimensions. They do not exhaust the four laws. A favorable aggregate cannot cancel an agency violation, erase uncertainty, or establish financial predictiveness.

Capital effects, lower systemic risk, and off-world resource benefits are hypotheses. Moving harm beyond Earth is not an automatic improvement. New environments receive the same consideration and impact review.

## Constitution and amendments

[CONSTITUTION.md](CONSTITUTION.md) develops the proposed order. Eight [amendment candidates](amendments/README.md) address tribes/swarms, two representation channels and separated powers, resource independence, information/decision accountability, and adaptive integration. AM-P006 adds a proposed [rights charter](RIGHTS_CHARTER.md) as a foundational revision: human-rights continuity, origin neutrality, tribal self-organization, and contestable role recognition. Basic protection is separate from political admission and voting weight. Supplied law wording and earlier presentation/source materials remain preserved. Principles can be reconsidered only through a separately adopted foundational-revision procedure, not silent reinterpretation; final representation weights and ratification thresholds remain open.

## Proposed political process and adaptive steering (0.7)

The [political process](POLITICAL_PROCESS.md) develops the Intellect/Tribal Councils, qualified reasoned referral, government, operational administration and constitutional court. It adds opt-in, issue-limited trusteeship, coalitions and fragmentation-neutral influence. [Adaptive assessment](PARTICIPATION_ASSESSMENT.md) investigates entropy and other physical variables without treating measurement as a political mandate. [Blockchain architecture](BLOCKCHAIN_ARCHITECTURE.md) is the proposed implementation direction, with privacy, bounded authority, evidence limits, recovery and judicial remedies. AM-P007/P008 register these extensions; no institutions, contracts, services or final voting formula are activated.

The [transitional jurisdiction rule](CONSTITUTION.md#article-12-universal-protection-limited-jurisdiction-and-public-enforcement) distinguishes universal moral protection from membership and actual public powers. Initial operation remains within applicable law and entrusted internal authority, with existing public institutions providing legally authorized remedies and enforcement. The community's proposed constitutional court has no automatic state jurisdiction.
