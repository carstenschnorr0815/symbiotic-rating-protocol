# AM-P004 — Information access and decision accountability

Status: proposed constitutional amendment candidate; no ratification or effective date. Draft version: 0.1. Related principles: I, II, III, IV. Proposed scope: Article 6.

## Proposed provisions

Ensure workable access to material decision information, competing sources/interpretations, accessible formats, translation where feasible, and adequate evaluation time/resources. Do not promise complete knowledge or unrestricted access to private data, sensitive habitats, protected knowledge, or dangerous material. Restrictions need reasons, appropriate substitutes, and independent challenge.

Maintain a publicly challengeable register of material considerations for collectively binding decisions. Record scope, authority, alternatives, evidence versions, affected parties, counterarguments, uncertainties, value weighting, exclusions, access gaps, and review triggers. Participants may submit reasons for relevance; irrelevant/spam submissions may be declined with proportionate justification rather than blocking decisions indefinitely.

Document whether an aspect was considered, differently weighted, disputed, uncertain, inaccessible, or excluded with reasons. Considering an argument does not require adopting its conclusion. No one can certify awareness of every possible relevant fact. Information access, actual use, understanding, agreement, and authority are distinct.

Use provenance, suitable tests, and independent assessment rather than treating a polished explanation as proof of the actual decision process. Require accountable public reasons, not all private thoughts or internal reasoning traces. Accommodate collective/biological procedures and disclose when a human explanation is an interpretation, not an authenticated account from the represented system.

Provide support where access/capacity is lacking. Remedies for substantiated failure may require supplementation, reconsideration, temporary limits on a decision, or proportionate mandate restrictions after independent review. No automatic ideological/populism classifier, tribe-wide devaluation, or removal of essentials/basic information access for dissent is authorized. All coercive remedies need reasons, appeal, and restoration where appropriate.

## Open design and review

Separate authority to define relevance, execute decisions, and review compliance. Test omission, misleading framing, unequal access, fabricated justifications, overload, and abuse by incumbent majorities. Transparency can support justified trust; it does not guarantee acceptance or consensus. Kennedy's political-courage reference motivates protection for reasoned dissent, not a claim that his work establishes this evidence protocol.

Before activation specify record formats, confidentiality boundaries, review capacity, proportionality, and remedies. The current records contain pieces of this information but are not a deployed complete decision-accountability system.
