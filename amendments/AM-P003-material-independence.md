# AM-P003 — Material independence of participation

Status: proposed constitutional amendment candidate; no ratification or effective date. Draft version: 0.1. Related principles: I, II, III. Proposed scope: Article 5.

## Proposed provisions

No already represented actor may unilaterally control another representation's essential existence, communication, or operational resources to direct its political conduct. Disclose actual control: ownership, financing, maintenance, configuration changes, memory, input/output filtering, identity replacement, and shutdown powers. A nominally different provider is not sufficient.

Provide shared, purpose-limited infrastructure with transparent budgets, separated operator/funder/review roles, continuity reserves, and feasible portability/fallback. Common democratic resource policy remains possible; targeted deprivation to suppress dissent is not routine administration. Apply the principle to biological essentials and habitats as well as AI operation.

Neither limitless computing claims nor unreviewable operation is granted. Necessary general limits, maintenance, or urgent danger responses need authority, reasons, proportionate scope, logging, time limits where appropriate, and independent appeal/review. Safety claims cannot be conclusively judged by the actor whose control is disputed. No entitlement to uncontrolled self-expansion follows from participation.

## Open design and review

Compare independent public-purpose operation, distributed providers, and other arrangements. Assess common control, key custody, energy/network dependency, funding loss, migration feasibility, and retaliation. Resource independence does not prove independent goals or authentic political positions; those remain separate questions.

Before activation define responsible institutions, budgets, continuity guarantees, legitimate suspension processes, and remedies. Review when control becomes concentrated, migration fails, or new embodiment/resource dependencies emerge. No real infrastructure trust or secured funding is created by this proposal.
