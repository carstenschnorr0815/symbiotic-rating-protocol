# AM-P006 — Rights, origin neutrality, and tribal recognition

Status: proposed **foundational-revision candidate**, draft 0.1; no ratification or effective date. Related principles: I–IV. Affected scope: proposed RIGHTS_CHARTER.md, constitutional Articles 1–2, 5–8, and participation/control-domain interpretation. Supplied law wording is unchanged.

## Purpose and proposed text

Adopt only through a separately authorized foundational-revision process the [rights charter](../RIGHTS_CHARTER.md). Its numbered provisions are the proposed text; accompanying framework edits form a consolidated proposed reading, not independent adoption. Add basic-rights constraints between principles and institutional implementation without diluting human protections.

Separate basic consideration, internal tribal membership/decision eligibility, and recognition for common political roles. Origin, instantiation, training, or former commissioning alone cannot establish permanent subordination. Present control, not ancestry, determines the relevance of common-control safeguards. Provide independent reassessment of evolved communities without automatically creating extra votes.

## Reasons, alternatives, and impacts

The UDHR provides the reference; extensions to other intellects and ecological protections are our proposals. Consciousness is not an admission test for contribution or mandate eligibility. Experience remains relevant to specific welfare harms. Preserve uncertainty rather than pretending uniform identity, rights application, or consent.

Alternatives include leaving rights dispersed across existing documents or requiring a human-like personhood threshold. Review their exclusion risks against this charter's risks of over-attributing identity, creating unbounded resource claims, or capturing admission. Tribal autonomy must remain compatible with constituent protection, accessible challenge, and legitimate restrictions of particular powers. No political admission condition may erase basic protection.

## Adoption, transition, and review

No separately adopted foundational-revision procedure, independent recognition authority, or final representation weights exist. The ordinary repository quorum and 30-day review minimum cannot establish this revision. Future adoption must identify exact charter text/commit, affected provisions, competent scope, transition duties, resources, dissent, and effective date. Neither this proposal nor its technical register authorizes that process itself.

Review continuity after copies/model changes, collective membership, genuine changes of control, material independence, scarce resources, jurisdiction, accessible remedies, and cultural assumptions with affected and diverse reviewers. Preserve basic protection during disputes and provisional recognition. No legal status, endorsement, or institutional mandate is asserted.
