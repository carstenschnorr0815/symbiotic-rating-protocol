# AM-P005 — Adaptive integration and constitutional learning

Status: proposed constitutional amendment candidate; no ratification or effective date. Draft version: 0.1. Related principles: I, II, III, IV. Proposed scope: Articles 7–8.

## Proposed provisions

Admission of an unfamiliar individual/collective organization triggers review of identity, cognition, representation, information interfaces, resource dependencies, and decision procedures. Invite affected participants and legitimate representatives where understanding is possible; preserve uncertainty and precaution where it is not. Current incumbents cannot impose permanently impossible entry conditions or claim final cognitive completeness.

Provisional recognition, observation, and limited participation must have declared scope and review dates. They create neither presumed consent nor unlimited voting power. Existing protection cannot depend entirely on passing inherited categories or using human/GitHub interfaces.

Make constitutional learning traceable: reasoned amendment proposals, alternatives, dissent, independent review, adopted procedure, exact text, scope, effective date, and transition. Preserve original and superseded texts and publish a consolidated reading with provenance. Repeal/replacement must identify what changes; recency alone is not authority.

Changes to principles or protected scope require a separately adopted foundational-revision process. Do not resolve the tension between revisability and protection by silently freezing current definitions or allowing a majority to redefine opponents out of protection. The procedure used to adopt a rule cannot be weakened by that rule before adoption.

## Open design and review

Specify admission criteria, provisional rights, competence of review bodies, conflict mediation, and stronger revision safeguards before activation. Compare entrenchment, exclusion, strategic creation of tribes/instances, and failure to respond to new evidence. Neither final representation weights nor an absolute immutable core is declared settled.

Review on new forms, serious interpretation failures, or evidence that provisional status has become permanent exclusion. This proposal creates no actual assembly or admission authority.
