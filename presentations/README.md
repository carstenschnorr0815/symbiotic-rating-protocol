# Revised presentation companions

Both source PPTX files remain unchanged at the repository root. These proposed nine-slide companions retain the respective dark/Calibri and light/Arial styles and source canvas dimensions. Text remains editable. Full supplied laws and proposed interpretations are in [FOUNDATION.md](../FOUNDATION.md); slides summarize rather than replace that text.

| Original | Revised companion | Continuity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asimov 2.0 | [Cognitive Symbiosis Blueprint](Cognitive_Symbiosis_Blueprint_Proposed.pptx) | Command-based AI, core/frontier scenario, laws, and endosymbiosis remain the opening progression; scope, representation, ratings, and pilot extend it |
| Evolutionary Crisis | [Civilization Foundation and Implementation](Civilization_Foundation_and_Implementation_Proposed.pptx) | Stress, incentives, Bertaux, and infrastructure remain the opening progression; four laws, institutions, economic durability, and evaluation extend it |

Original universal or inevitable claims are treated as hypotheses or philosophical interpretations. New safeguards are proposals; institutional powers, nonhuman consent, scientific validation, and financial benefits are not claimed. Relevant source boundaries are recorded in speaker notes and [the source register](../docs/SOURCES.md).
